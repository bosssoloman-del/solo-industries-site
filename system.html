§ 01 — Infrastructure

SGIE-SFL

Self-Generating IP Expansion, sustained by the Self-Sustained Feedback Loop, governed by Ma’at, executed by Elaine and Da Nine.

The doubled “self” is deliberate. Self-generating means it creates without the Founder in the room. Self-sustained means it keeps going without him. Those are the two obligations of a company whose founder is invisible by design.

The cycle runs knowledge → IP → products → feedback → memory → new knowledge. It does not return where it started. It returns higher. That climb is the expansion, and it is why the mark is a spiral.

§ 02 — Governance

Ma’at — seven principles, enforced in code

Ma’at is the Kemetic principle of right order. Here it is implemented, not quoted.

OrderStructure before speed. Every decision has a place it lives.
BalanceNo line carries the whole weight. Six businesses, one system.
TruthSay the number. Never dress a failure as a result.
JusticeWhat is owed gets paid. To workers, to partners, to the record.
HarmonyThe parts serve the whole. No subsidiary wins at another's cost.
ReciprocityValue in, value out. The loop returns what it is given.
ProprietyDo it right when nobody is watching. Especially then.

Green

Reads, drafts, research, summaries, customer answers from verified material. Runs immediately.

Amber

Publishing, external sends, record changes, bulk actions. Prepared and staged — held for the Founder.

Red

Moving money, filing legal documents, deletions, anything irreversible. Refused outright.
