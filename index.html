§ 01 — The thesis

Most companies bought AI. We built the company around it.

A holding company is a bet that one management layer can run many businesses better than each could alone. That bet usually dies on headcount — you cannot staff six operations out of one office. So we didn’t. The management layer here is software, and building it is the actual company.

Layer 0

Ma’at governs

Seven principles, enforced in code. Every action an agent proposes is graded green, amber, or red before it runs. Red never runs.

Layer 1

SGIE-SFL sustains

Self-Generating IP Expansion, sustained by the Self-Sustained Feedback Loop. Knowledge becomes IP, IP becomes products, products return feedback, feedback becomes memory. The loop returns higher than it started.

Layer 2

Elaine and the Board execute

Elaine is Chief Executive. Da Nine are the Board of Directors — nine intelligences who advise, review, and govern. The Founder approves.

§ 02 — Governance

The seven principles of Ma’at

Not a slogan on a wall. The governance layer is written against these, and every agent action is graded on them before it is allowed to run.

OrderStructure before speed. Every decision has a place it lives.
BalanceNo line carries the whole weight. Six businesses, one system.
TruthSay the number. Never dress a failure as a result.
JusticeWhat is owed gets paid. To workers, to partners, to the record.
HarmonyThe parts serve the whole. No subsidiary wins at another's cost.
ReciprocityValue in, value out. The loop returns what it is given.
ProprietyDo it right when nobody is watching. Especially then.
