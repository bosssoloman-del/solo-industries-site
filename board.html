Leadership

Elaine is Chief Executive.
Da Nine are the Board.

Eleven agents run this company. Elaine holds the chief executive seat and the front door. Da Nine sit as the Board of Directors — they advise, review, and govern under Ma’at. The Founder approves. That is the whole chain of command.

Talk to the CEO

Chief Executive

Elaine

Executive intelligence and the front door of the company. She interprets what you need, decides which director owns it, hands it off, and returns one clean answer instead of nine partial ones. She is also the chat on this site — same agent, same system, no demo version.

What she will not do

Move money. File documents. Publish externally. Delete anything. Name the Founder. Invent a number. Those require recorded approval, every time, without exception — and some of them are refused outright no matter who asks.

Board of Directors
