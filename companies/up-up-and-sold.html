On the surface it is resale. Underneath, it is the sharpest data source in the portfolio.

The business

Collectibles, gaming, and resale — sourced, graded, listed, moved.

The real reason

Collectibles markets reprice in public, in real time, across a hundred listings a day. Watching that closely teaches the system what demand actually looks like before it shows up anywhere slower. What this line learns about pricing and timing goes into the loop, and apparel and publishing get to use it.
